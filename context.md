# CIPP / AIGP Practice Quiz App — Context

## Purpose
A simple webapp for practicing multiple-choice exam questions for the IAPP
certifications:
- **CIPP/US** (US private-sector privacy law)
- **CIPP/E** (European data protection law / GDPR)
- **AIGP** (AI Governance Professional)

Public site: any visitor can take the quiz and get scored, anonymously, no
sign-up required. The app owner is the only one who manages the question
bank and can see their own persistent history (see Persistence & Access
below).

## Core Features (v1)
- **Instant feedback + explanations**: after answering a question, immediately
  show correct/incorrect and a short explanation of the right answer.
- **Domain/topic filtering**: practice by specific exam domain (e.g. just
  "US Privacy Law", "AI Governance Lifecycle") instead of the whole bank,
  per certification.
- **Score history / weak-area tracking**: past quiz attempts are saved and
  viewable over time, with a breakdown of accuracy per domain to highlight
  weak areas.

Not in v1 (explicitly deferred): timed exam simulation mode.

## Question Bank
- Multiple-choice format.
- Starter set generated by Claude based on public IAPP exam outlines for
  CIPP/US, CIPP/E, and AIGP — **larger starter size**, roughly 25–30
  questions per domain, organized by each certification's official domain
  structure.
- Content is a starting point; expected to be edited/expanded by the user
  over time (exact editing mechanism — e.g. admin UI vs. editing a data
  file/DB directly — TBD at implementation time).

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: modern framework — React with Vite (or Next.js, TBD at
  implementation time).
- **Backend/DB**: Supabase (Postgres) for storing the question bank and the
  user's quiz/score history durably.
- **Hosting**: deployed to Vercel, accessible via a URL from any device.
- **Auth**: light single-user authentication (e.g. Supabase auth with one
  account) — just enough to secure the data as belonging to the owner, not a
  multi-user system.

## Persistence & Access
- **Owner (you)**: durable, cross-device history — your quiz history and
  scores must survive clearing browser data or switching browsers/devices,
  via the hosted Supabase DB. Light single-user auth secures this data as
  yours.
- **Public visitors**: anonymous, session-only. Anyone can visit, take a
  quiz, and see their own score at the end. No login, no nickname capture,
  no cross-visit history. Their individual results are not stored/reviewable
  by the owner and are not shown to other visitors — no leaderboard.
- **Question bank editing**: only the owner can add/edit questions.
  Visitors can only take quizzes.
