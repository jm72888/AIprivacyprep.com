# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences:
- **App owner** (the developer/user themselves): studying for the IAPP
  CIPP/US, CIPP/E, and AIGP certifications. Needs durable, cross-device quiz
  history to track study progress over time, and is the only one who curates
  the question bank.
- **Public visitors**: anyone who wants free practice questions for these
  certifications. Anonymous, no account, takes a quiz and sees their own
  score at the end.

## Product Purpose

A practice quiz app for the IAPP CIPP/US, CIPP/E, and AIGP certifications.
Gives instant feedback with explanations after each answer, lets users filter
practice by exam domain, and tracks score history so weak domains become
visible over time. Success means someone using it can identify and close
gaps in their exam prep.

## Positioning

Free, no-signup access to practice questions across all three IAPP
certifications in one app — no paid course or account required to start
practicing immediately.

## Operating Context

Web app. Core workflow: pick a certification (and optionally filter to
specific domains) → answer multiple-choice questions with instant
feedback/explanation per question → view results with a per-domain accuracy
breakdown. The owner additionally reviews persistent history across past
attempts over time.

Phase 1 (current): fully working against local data (`src/data/`), quiz
attempt history stored in the browser's `localStorage`.

Phase 2 (planned, not started): swap the local data client for a
Supabase-backed implementation, add light single-user auth for the owner,
deploy to Vercel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multiple-choice questions with instant correct/incorrect feedback and a
  short explanation shown immediately after answering.
- Domain/topic filtering within a certification.
- Score history with per-domain accuracy breakdown, to surface weak areas.
- Public visitors: fully anonymous and session-only — no login, no nickname
  capture, no cross-visit history, results not stored or reviewable by the
  owner, no leaderboard.
- Owner: durable cross-device history (Phase 2, via Supabase + light
  single-user auth); only the owner can add/edit questions, visitors can
  only take quizzes.
- Explicitly deferred from v1: timed exam simulation mode.
- Undecided: exact mechanism for the owner to edit/expand the question bank
  after the starter set (admin UI vs. editing data/DB directly) — TBD at
  implementation time.

## Evidence on Hand

- Starter question bank (~25–30 questions per domain) in
  `src/data/questions/*.ts`, generated by Claude from public IAPP exam
  outlines. This is a starting point for the owner to edit/expand, not
  official or licensed IAPP content — accuracy is not guaranteed and the app
  has no affiliation with IAPP.
- `supabase/schema.sql`: planned Phase 2 schema (certifications, domains,
  questions, attempts, attempt_answers) with row-level security policies
  matching the public-read / owner-only-write access model above.
- `/Design` folder: reference images for visual style inspiration only, not
  literal layouts (visual-world evidence for DESIGN.md/new-work, not detailed
  further here).

## Product Principles

- Free and open access — no signup or paywall to take a practice quiz.
- The owner is the sole content curator — visitors consume the question
  bank, only the owner edits it.
- Practice-first, not exam simulation — instant feedback and explanations
  take priority over timed, high-stakes testing.
- Weak-area visibility — persistent per-domain score tracking exists so the
  owner can see what to restudy, not just a final score.
- Content is a living starting point — the Claude-generated starter bank is
  expected to be corrected and expanded, not treated as final or
  authoritative.

## Brand Commitments
